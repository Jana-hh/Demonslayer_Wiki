❖ ABOUT DEMON SLAYER

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba is a Japanese anime series based on the manga written and illustrated by Koyoharu Gotouge. It follows Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy whose life changes after demons attack his family. His younger sister, Nezuko, survives but becomes a demon. Determined to protect her and find a way to restore her humanity, Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps and begins a dangerous journey.

Along the way, Tanjiro meets fellow demon slayers and powerful Hashira who help him face increasingly dangerous enemies. The series explores themes of family, courage, compassion, determination, and the struggle between humans and demons.
