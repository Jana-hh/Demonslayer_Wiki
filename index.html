Demon Slayer

Kimetsu no Yaiba follows the journey of Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy living in Taishō-era Japan whose life is shattered when his family is attacked by Muzan Kibutsuji, the original and most powerful demon. His younger sister, Nezuko, is transformed into a demon but retains her human emotions and protective instincts toward her brother. Determined to find a cure for Nezuko and protect others, Tanjiro trains to join the Demon Slayer Corps, an organization dedicated to hunting demons. Alongside Zenitsu Agatsuma and Inosuke Hashibira, he faces dangerous missions and powerful enemies. They eventually work with the Hashira, the strongest swordsmen of the Corps, to fight Muzan and the Twelve Kizuki. Their journey leads to a final battle inside the Infinity Castle, where the Demon Slayers fight to protect humanity.
